ABOUT TERRAGUARD AI

Technology Supporting Environmental Awareness

Landslides can create serious challenges for communities, infrastructure and transportation. Understanding environmental conditions can help people become more aware of potential hazards.

TerraGuard AI is an academic web development prototype that combines weather information, geographical data visualization and AI-assisted insights in one platform.
