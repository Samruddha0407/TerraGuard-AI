Settings & Assessment Information

How TerraGuard collects data and assigns screening levels

TRANSPARENCY

Screening score bands

  • Low0–24 / 85
  • Moderate25–44 / 85
  • High45–64 / 85
  • Severe65–85 / 85

Factors considered

Recent 24-hour precipitation, accumulated 7-day precipitation, relative humidity and selected heavy-rain or thunderstorm weather codes.

Terrain, slope angle, soil type, geology, drainage, land use and field sensor observations are not included in this prototype score.

Data sources

Weather: Open-Meteo forecast API

Location search: Open-Meteo geocoding API

Map: OpenStreetMap tiles, with Esri fallback

Storage: MongoDB when configured; temporary in-memory mode otherwise

Important safety notice

This is an academic screening prototype, not a validated landslide prediction model and not an official warning service. Weather indicators alone cannot confirm or rule out slope failure.

In an emergency: move away from slopes and follow local disaster-management and government instructions. Do not wait for this dashboard.

Data may be delayed or unavailable. Always verify conditions with official local authorities.
